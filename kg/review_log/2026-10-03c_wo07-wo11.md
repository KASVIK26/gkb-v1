  are statements about the whole population; the model attached them to one pathotype (238S119, pathotype 11). Not asserted.
- Kept the 52-6 statements and added Lr39: "pathotype 52-6 ... possesses additional virulence against Lr24 and Lr39 while remaining avirulent to Lr26" names the pathotype.
- One pathotype, one name: the genome paper writes `Pst46S119`, the newsletters `46S119`; both are now `Pst 46S119` (Pgt 11, Pt 52-4 ...: a prefix so that "11" is not a bare number).

- WO07-0014 was rejected by the verifier only because a page header ("Mehtaensis 40(2): July, 2020 Page 3") falls inside the sentence; quote rebuilt with the cut marked.

**WO08**

## Tooling changes made during this review (tested)

1. Crossref fallback (`curator/lit/crossref.py`): a DOI Europe PMC does not index is verified against Crossref (title, year, venue) and its quote is searched on the publisher's page and linked PDFs; not found = "unverifiable", never "verified".
2. `quote_audit.fetch_source_text` now falls back to the publisher's page for such DOIs, so the final audit can re-check them (one new test).
3. New pathotypes take their pathogen from the claim (a Yr/Lr/Sr/Pm gene's pathogen, or the "variant of" object); advisories are one practice = one entity whose id does not name a disease; the dose-number check uses whole numbers.
4. `tests/test_kg_files.py::test_production_tracks_a_defeated_gene` now also expects the two undated 52-6 claims.

## Known limits

- One source per claim for most of these, so most are tier C/D by construction; the pathotype claims for 238S119 and pathotype 11 have two independent newsletters.
- The AESA wheat IPM package is from 2014 and the CIBRC table is a registration list, not a recommendation for a specific field: both are "what the label allows", with no pathotype or season.
- Pathotype names are not scoped to a country in the source; "dominant in India" is not a claim here, only that the pathotype exists and belongs to its pathogen.
