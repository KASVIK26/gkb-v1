# WO10 Leads

- Full text of Frontiers 2021 (PMID 34149753) already opened; additional time-point gene-expression data available for deeper triggers.
- Journal of Agrometeorology 24(2) soybean anthracnose full PDF: more precise Tmax/Tmin/RHm ranges and correlation coefficients.
- Multiple regression models for stripe rust (Hisar, Karnal, Gurdaspur) with exact equations – useful for sensor algorithms.
- Charcoal-rot soil-temperature (5 cm / 10 cm) values from IJCMAS 2020 need full table verification.
- Chickpea Fusarium wilt quantitative models (Landa et al. Phytopathology) give soil-temperature optima 22-28 °C by race – classic reference, open access.
- Leaf-wetness duration studies for wheat rusts in India remain scarce; most Indian work is correlative rather than controlled wetness-hour thresholds.
- Central-India (Malwa) specific multi-year weather × disease datasets for soybean and chickpea still thin in open literature.
