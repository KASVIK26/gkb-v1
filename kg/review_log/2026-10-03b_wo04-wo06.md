## Defects in my own tooling found while doing this (fixed, tested)

1. A table's caption was appended after all table rows in the audited text, so any correct "caption ... header ... row" quote failed the in-order check.
2. Table cell separators (` | `) were part of the comparison on one side only; they are now neutral on both.
3. A strict schema rejected a candidate for an extra key in its `source` block; unknown keys inside `source` are now ignored (everywhere else extra keys are still errors, so misspelt qualifiers are caught).
4. A real bracket in the paper's text ("*Glycine max* [L.] Merr.") was treated as an editorial cut; a quote that matches as written is now accepted first.
5. A new QTL needs a trait; for `QTL_ASSOCIATION` the trait is the disease in the claim itself, now the default.

## Known limits

- Everything here is one source per claim, and most of it a review article's statement; treat tier D as "plausible, listed in the literature", not "established".
- Gene names such as `Yr2ks`, `h1`, `FOC-2` are as the source writes them; no cross-catalogue reconciliation (Komugi / McIntosh) was done.
- The wheat table in pmid:33013989 compiles older literature; a variety's listed genes may not be the current best estimate. The 6 WO05 candidates the verifier first rejected were the HD2189 rows; they are restored by repairing the quote.
