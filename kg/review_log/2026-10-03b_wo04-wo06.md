## Decisions that changed the model's output

- **WO04 catalogue / WO06 chickpea meta-QTLs**: the model's quotes glued a merged-cell disease heading to a later row, reordered columns, or omitted cells. Quotes rebuilt as
  `caption ... header ... disease cell ... the row exactly as printed`. Values were not changed; all matched.
- **WO04 donor species**: not recorded when the cell is "Triticum spp." (a genus placeholder, not a species) or misspelt in the source (Leymus "rasomsus"); a source typo is not copied into data.
- **WO04 aliases** made of words literally in the quote: "leaf" / "yellow" (Lr34, Lr46, Lr67 "provide resistance to leaf, yellow and stem rust and powdery mildew"), "pachyrhizi", "SMV", "pustule",
  "chickpea wilt" (the table caption's name for the disease).
- **WO04 chickpea wilt genes**: the race each gene acts against is recorded in `spectrum` from the row (race 1A h1/h2/H3 "late wilting"; races 2-5 "complete resistance"); the source's own gene names
  (h1, h2, H3, FOC-2 ...) are kept. The model's "Table 1." label was not in the caption and was removed.
- **WO04 vs the KB**: 9 new `resistance_type: unknown` claims were dropped because the KB already holds the same gene-disease pair with a specific type (Lr34 x3, Lr26, Sr35, Fhb1, Fhb7, Pm6, Pm37).
- **WO05 evidence type**: five rows from pmid:40678044 were typed `marker`; the table is captioned "Inferred presence" and the paper derives it from seedling reactions against isogenic lines
  (gene postulation), so they are `postulation` (weaker). `HD2189`: the source prints its gene cells in the order Lr, Sr, Yr; the model had reordered them to match the header, so the quote was restored.
- **WO06 stripe-rust QTL**: the model's numbers were right (LOD 8.0 / PVE 34.1 and 2.6 / 20.1); only the quote was not verbatim (it skipped the interval-size cell). Rebuilt from the table text.
- **Dropped**: WO06 GWAS marker rows (WO06-0042..45: low value, header carries footnote letters) and the soybean Rpp SNP rows (WO06-0053..56: SNP names contain commas, and the model's sentence skipped parts
  of the source without marking the cut).
- Display names: `HD2285` -> `HD 2285`, `SONALIKA` -> `Sonalika` (ids unchanged).

## Defects in my own tooling found while doing this (fixed, tested)

1. A table's caption was appended after all table rows in the audited text, so any correct "caption ... header ... row" quote failed the in-order check.
2. Table cell separators (` | `) were part of the comparison on one side only; they are now neutral on both.
3. A strict schema rejected a candidate for an extra key in its `source` block; unknown keys inside `source` are now ignored (everywhere else extra keys are still errors, so misspelt qualifiers are caught).
4. A real bracket in the paper's text ("*Glycine max* [L.] Merr.") was treated as an editorial cut; a quote that matches as written is now accepted first.
5. A new QTL needs a trait; for `QTL_ASSOCIATION` the trait is the disease in the claim itself, now the default.

## Known limits

- Everything here is one source per claim, and most of it a review article's statement; treat tier D as "plausible, listed in the literature", not "established".
- Gene names such as `Yr2ks`, `h1`, `FOC-2` are as the source writes them; no cross-catalogue reconciliation (Komugi / McIntosh) was done.
- The wheat table in pmid:33013989 compiles older literature; a variety's listed genes may not be the current best estimate. The 6 WO05 candidates the verifier first rejected were the HD2189 rows; they are restored by repairing the quote.
